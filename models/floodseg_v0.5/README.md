Radar flood segmentation model v0.5 of hackathon2-povodne: U-Net ResNet34, fine-tuned from v0.4 on Kuro Siwo train events + Sen1Floods11 (without Sri Lanka and Bolivia), hard-tile oversampling, event-balanced sampling.
Kuro Siwo: Bountos et al., 2024 (CC BY 4.0). Sen1Floods11: Bonafilia et al., 2020. ImageNet-pretrained encoder.
