# Demo video script (3:00) — filmed by the user
No images of victims anywhere. Voice-over in English. Screen: dashboard in dark theme. Prepare a finished Trishuli run in History beforehand (a live run takes minutes); show the live start, then cut to the finished run.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Title slide, then map of Nepal | "On 26 August 2026 a glacier collapse sent a flood down the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli valley. Rescuers need three answers: where did it hit, what is damaged, who is cut off. We built a system that answers them from free satellite data." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Draw a rectangle (about 30×30 km) around the valley; type date 2026-08-26; open *Advanced* briefly (sensitivity, before/after windows) | "Pick any area and any flood date — nothing is hard-coded for Nepal. The system finds and downloads Sentinel-1 radar from the same orbit before and after, optical images if clear, elevation, and OpenStreetMap as it was before the flood." |
| 0:40–0:55 | Press Run; stepper shows steps; cut to finished run | "It runs end to end on a laptop CPU: download, preprocessing, AI flood model, debris step, damage, road network, report." |
| 0:55–1:25 | Verdict + data-quality panel; flood water and debris layers; before/after slider; click a debris polygon to show the evidence sentence | "Flood water comes from a U-Net trained on Kuro Siwo and Sen1Floods11. Mud and debris, which do not look like water in radar, come from a separate change test with a confidence level. The data-quality panel shows what the satellites could actually see." |
| 1:25–1:50 | Damaged buildings, blocked roads, suspect bridges; show numbers as range | "Buildings, roads and bridges from OpenStreetMap before the event are checked one by one. We show ranges, and a bridge over flooded water is only marked suspect." |
| 1:50–2:20 | Settlements green / orange / red; click a cut-off village: blocking section, detour, walking time; urgency list; repair plan; landing-zone candidates (labelled unverified) | "Who is cut off? We rebuild the road graph before and after. Red villages have no road to a town or hospital. The system ranks them by urgency and shows which road section to repair first." |
| 2:20–2:35 | Click a point upstream: flood path and settlements along it; then download the one-page situation report | "Bonus: click any point to trace the flood path downstream. And a one-page situation report is generated from the same statistics file." |
| 2:35–2:55 | Model card slide: Kuro Siwo F1 0.79; whole pipeline on 12 independent floods mean F1 0.81; Bolivia 0.20 without before image; Trishuli vs EMSR927: precision 0.92 / recall 0.79; weak on mountain flash floods; *Methods & Limitations* dialog | "We report weak spots honestly: the model is a change detector, mountain recall is low, narrow rivers are invisible to radar, satellites revisit every few days and cannot warn ahead of a sudden collapse. This is an educational prototype, not an operational tool." |
| 2:55–3:00 | Attributions slide | "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026; © OpenStreetMap contributors; Copernicus DEM; Kuro Siwo, Sen1Floods11. Built with Claude Code." |
